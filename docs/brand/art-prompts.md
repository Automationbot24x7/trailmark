# Illustration

`trail-art.png` (a path of paper invoices and receipts with one stretch broken away, a figure with a lantern at the gap, the bank on the far hill) was made with an AI image model from the prompt below. `card.html` lays the title on a paper label over it for the README hero (`docs/hero.jpg`) and the link preview (`web/og.png`). Screenshots and diagrams in `docs/` are rendered from the app and from code.

> Layered paper-cut art scene with real depth, like a shadow-box made of many layers of cut cream, ink-grey and vermilion paper: a winding footpath made of overlapping paper documents (purchase orders, receipts, invoices, bank statements) climbs through layered paper hills toward a small bank building with columns on the far hill. Partway along, one section of the path has broken away, leaving a gap over a dark ravine; a tiny figure holding a glowing lantern stands at the edge looking at the gap. Soft lighting between the layers, rich detail, calm and serious mood. Wide 3:2 landscape, scene in the lower two thirds, open layered sky across the top third.
