# Illustration

`trail-art.png` (the paper trail with the cut red thread) was made with an AI image model from the prompt below, then composed with the title in `card.html` (README hero `docs/hero.jpg`, link preview `web/og.png`). Everything else in `docs/` is drawn or rendered from code.

> Overhead flat-lay of a row of five paper business documents laid left to right on a warm off-white paper surface: a purchase order sheet, a small goods receipt slip, an invoice, a bank cheque, and a long bank statement strip. A single vermilion red thread runs from document to document, pinned to each one, but between the second and third document the thread is cut: two loose ends curl apart leaving a clear gap. The documents show only abstract ruled lines, boxes and columns, never readable writing. Calm, precise, serious mood for an accounting tool. Limited palette: black ink, warm cream paper, one vermilion red accent. Wide 3:2 landscape composition, documents in the lower two thirds, generous empty cream space across the top third. Style: fine-line editorial ink illustration with flat colour fills, crisp outlines, subtle soft shadows, like a quality financial newspaper illustration.
